Before you head out

Every card links to the venue or its source. “Fixed-date” means its date/time was checked against a current official listing or published seasonal schedule. It does not guarantee tickets, equipment, or last-minute availability. “Flexible” means choose a day within opening hours, not necessarily walk in without booking.

What changed from the original chat?
  • Teen Open Lab’s current break-week sessions could not be confirmed. Do not plan around the earlier Tue–Thu times. Check with Urbana Free Library; Douglass is the confirmed maker alternative.
  • The Allerton collage intensive is Oct 17–18, not Sunday alone, and its Oct 10 registration deadline has passed. It is omitted from bookable picks. Official listing.
  • Friday planetarium show titles/times, the ice-skate session, Hot Dogs and Goats, acro yoga, bird walks, the flea market, and the Scratching Post art/knitting events were not confirmed for this window and are not advertised as scheduled outings.
  • Giertz Gallery has no Friday or Sunday hours in the current fall announcement. The special Saturday planetarium pick is explicitly in Spanish.
  • Other unconfirmed downtown events were left out of the dated list. This guide favors verified plans over a crowded calendar.

Bring a layer for outdoor stops. Arrange rides for Savoy, north Champaign and Allerton. For paid activities, check the total and any minor/guardian requirements before reserving. Food preferences and mobility needs can shape the plan.
